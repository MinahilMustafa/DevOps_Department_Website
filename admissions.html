Admissions

Join our community of innovators. Find everything you need to apply.

Application Steps

1. Online Application

Submit your application form through the university portal and pay the application fee.

2. Submit Transcripts

Provide official transcripts from your high school or previous educational institutions.

3. Entrance Exam

Take the university's entrance examination for Computer Science candidates.

4. Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an online or in-person interview.

Admission Criteria

  • Minimum 70% in high school (or equivalent)
  • Strong background in Mathematics
  • Passing score in the university entrance exam

Important Deadlines

  • Fall Application: July 15, 2026
  • Spring Application: November 15, 2026
  • Financial Aid: June 30, 2026
